# Data availability, provenance and limitations

Historical aggregates are transcribed from Tables 6–8, Section 5.1, Section 6.4 and the funding statement of the supplied JIAE manuscript. The 2026 dashboard CSV is from the supplied related PeerJ package and is consistent with the supplied aggregate dashboard workbook. The post-study dataset index reflects an author-supplied Zenodo listing, not an independent repository snapshot. Public venture source pointers are retained from the earlier source register; they have not been newly verified for this release.

The management clarification CSVs summarize information supplied on 26 November 2026. Their sources, scope and limitations are identified in each row and `OPERATIONAL_GOVERNANCE.md`. They are not inputs to the 2019–2025 aggregate calculations. No new formal human-participant study was conducted for this package; the routine feedback archive has not been coded or independently analyzed. No identifiable interview content is distributed. Any future use of underlying human-participant material requires appropriate institutional/ethics authorization and consent or other applicable basis.

Individual project files, scheduler logs, participant rosters, recordings, agreements and financial ledgers are not included. Access to original records depends on institutional permission, confidentiality and data protection obligations. Templates are unfilled and do not count as evidence. The private source archive remains outside the repository. The final manuscript and response letter are separate editorial documents and are not distributed here.
