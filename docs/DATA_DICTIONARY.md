# Counting rules

Study interval: 2019-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Annual innovation data are reported Table 6 activity counts, not a cohort-level transition matrix. A problem case requires a documented problem, context, stakeholder and intended digital response; dataset-enabled requires structured usable data and minimal metadata; laboratory-tested requires documented functional testing; field-tested requires an external/near-operational pilot report. The startup-pathway register records named emerging pathways and technical lineage; formal gate evidence and case crosswalk are not established. Three relative to 50 is a descriptive comparison, not a verified conversion. The aggregate complement of five relative to 50 is 90% under the nested-stage assumption; this is not a permanent failure rate. No additional cases are inferred from later releases.

`infrastructure_units`: historically reported inventory; not consistently physical nodes. `reported_active_users`: reported annual users/accounts; unique-person status and service-account exclusion unknown. `completed_jobs`: reported annual completed workloads; extraction rule not independently verified. `jobs_per_reported_user`: annual completed jobs divided by recorded active users; not individual engagement or utilization. `aggregate_accounting_hours`: historical heterogeneous CPU-core/GPU-device sum retained for traceability only, not comparable resource consumption, elapsed time, FLOPs, workload intensity or utilization. `training_participations`: annual roster-based reported counts; repeated participants may occur. Funding entries are reported portfolio amounts, not annual operating income; blank amount indicates no reported monetary value. `case_id` in the startup register is deliberately empty pending crosswalk.

`data/management_clarifications/`: reported approximate interview interaction counts and recorded subset (not unique people; period not established), reported 2025 financing shares in percent (not computed from ledgers), and qualitative descriptions of operational practice. None is an independently verified observation or included in historical output arithmetic. `data/context_2026/`: overlapping later portfolio window, excluded from historical calculations. Blank templates are schemas only.
